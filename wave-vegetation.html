Pre study of
wave-vegetation interaction

Abstract

This project is a preliminary numerical study of how submerged coastal vegetation influences wave propagation. The first model considers regular waves travelling through an array of rigid vertical cylinders. Linear wave theory is used to calculate the orbital flow, while Morison's equation estimates the hydrodynamic loading and the energy dissipated by vegetation drag. A wave-energy balance is then solved along the vegetated area to predict the progressive attenuation of wave height.

The study supports an application for a PhD scholarship on wave-vegetation interactions at DTU. Its purpose is to demonstrate the ability to select suitable physical models, handle coupled equations and develop a numerical methodology for this type of problem. The work is still in progress: the present model is preliminary and not yet fully validated, and its assumptions, implementation and results may evolve as the study develops.
